## Follow-up priority and limits

The selected loops show progress or an explicit stop/backpressure branch. No growing synchronous production iterator was established. Their neighboring retention policies do not all give a small process-wide byte ceiling.

The strongest new ownership lead is the Codex prompt registry's separate claims map. Confirmed cancellation deliberately retains a claim pending turn completion; both bounded lookup maps can evict that prompt, after which clearTurn cannot discover it. A bounded actual-source lifetime proof is needed before promoting a leak verdict or a fix. The answer-byte accounting, full command conversion, serialized plugin manifest duplication and detached worktree discard ownership are additional scope limits, not proven incident mechanisms.

The certificate accepted-identity map has a security purpose and follows Electron session lifetime. Its uncapped historical endpoint count is not authorization to clear it on guest retirement or add an arbitrary eviction policy.

Neither #19831 nor other open issue incidents are identified by these static checks. This batch introduces no host-data prerequisite and makes no claim about bytes retained on an affected host.
